8.1.1 Permanova analysis

Because both the overdispersion and PERMANOVA result are significant, we cannot conclude that one is because of significant differences or due to differences in dispersion between habitats. However, a PERMANOVA is considered to be robust against heterogeneous dispersions when the sample design is balanced as is the case in this study.

Anderson, M. J., & Walsh, D. C. (2013). PERMANOVA, ANOSIM, and the Mantel test in the face of heterogeneous dispersions: what null hypothesis are you testing?. Ecological monographs, 83(4), 557-574.
