9.4 Sensitivity Analysis

Following reviewer’s suggestion, compare the differences in GIFT score for assemblages in either habitat using all MAGs and exclusively high quality MAGs. This sensitivity analysis is intended to evaluate whether GIFT differences from aggregating incomplete genomes (medium quality; <90% completeness) creates artificial bias and false negatives

With unadjusted raw GIFT scores, medium-quality MAGs will appear artificially deficient in metabolic potential compared to high-quality MAGs which can impact their conclusions. When using to.community to aggregate MAG-specific GIFT scores into sample-level functional profiles, the completeness of individual MAGs directly influences the resulting community score. If a sample is dominated by MAGs with lower completeness, that sample’s community GIFT score will appear lower than a sample dominated by near-complete MAGs, even if their underlying functional capabilities are identical.
